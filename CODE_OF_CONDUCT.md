# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project welcomes respectful, constructive participation. Contributors must:

- Discuss the work, not the person.
- Give specific feedback and remain open to correction.
- Respect privacy, employment boundaries, and confidential information.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and unwanted personal contact.
- Stop an interaction when another participant asks for space.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Personal attacks or insulting language
- Sexualized language or attention
- Publishing another person's private information
- Sustained disruption of project discussions
- Deliberate misuse of employer, customer, or personal information

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that
violate this standard. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

To report conduct concerns privately, use
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/CRSD-Lau/Personal-Site/security/advisories/new).
Reports will be reviewed with discretion.

## Attribution

This policy is adapted for a small personal project from common open-source community standards.
