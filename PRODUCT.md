# Career portfolio

Author: Neil Mitchell
Last modified by: Neil Mitchell

## Register

brand

## Users

Professional contacts, hiring managers, and technical peers reviewing Neil Mitchell's
career, delivery experience, and independent engineering work.

## Product purpose

Present a memorable personal career portfolio, with clear access to current responsibilities,
five TD roles, supported impact measures, independent case studies, contact information,
and the current résumé.

## Brand personality

Personal, precise, practical. The experience should communicate professional confidence
and show the person behind the work.

## Anti-references

Avoid a corporate employer microsite, unsupported achievement claims, decorative skill
scores, or a technology showcase that obscures the career story.

## Design principles

- Give the professional identity and résumé an immediate, clear place.
- Use the real portrait and original project artwork to make the portfolio personal.
- Keep professional work and independent projects clearly attributed.
- Make the initial read concise while retaining the full career record.
- Keep all essential content readable in the static export.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, narrow viewports, light and dark themes,
reduced motion, and reading without JavaScript. Preserve all existing section anchors.
Use semantic disclosure controls for optional career detail.
